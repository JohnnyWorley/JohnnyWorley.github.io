Primeval Game

Freelance Project

Primeval game is a web based story puzzle game made in collaboration with Nu-Core Band Tallah.
The game follows the story told in their album Primeval Obsession//Detachment.
You play as Anna, a nurse on a planet called Nebrar who has been called to the temple to be given a task by The Elders, a task you cannot complete, but to refuse The Elders is death.

The game serves primarily as a medium for telling the story of the album, because of this the gameplay is a lot more focused on exploration and puzzle solving. The player can use a cultists mask to solve a puzzle to open a door, or use special glyphs to correctly light a room. Throughout the player also encounters various minigames.

  • Interactive Puzzles that involve exploring the environment to search for clues.
  • Skill Check Minigame sequence.

While making Primeval I was the Lead Programmer and Designer, working alongside my friend who was the Lead Artist. It was just us 2 so many aspects of the game were worked on by both of us, including level design, gameplay design, lighting, and UI. However all programming was done by me, and the same for his art. I also was the main avenue for communication between the development team, the band, and the publishing label, managing professional communications and ensuring that information was passed around effectively.

Since the game was being developed for the web and we wanted it to be able to run on any sort of machine, high or low end, optimisation was key. We took many approaches for optimisation. Much of the game is event drive, avoiding the Update loop as much as possible, this includes all the interaction, movement, and dialogue. Models were also highly optimised, with a low poly count and correct topology. We avoided adding unnecessary geometry where we could with things such as transparent/masked planes for certain objects.

Since the game had a PSX era art style, the resolution of the game was also decreased, alongside the render scale. This helped match the visual style we were aiming for while also drastically improving performance. We tested the game on many different devices, with our main focus being old, slow laptops, all of which were able to run the game with no issues.

We utilized LOD for models that were further away, especially in the outside scenes. With these heavily repeated models we also made use of GPU Instancing.
